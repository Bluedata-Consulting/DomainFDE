# Returns and Refunds Policy

## 1. Standard returns window

Unopened items in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a
full refund. The 30-day clock starts at the carrier's delivery scan, not at the order
date. Items returned on day 31 or later are refused at the warehouse and shipped back
to the customer at Aurora's cost.

## 2. Opened personal care items

Opened personal care items are returnable only within 7 days of delivery and only where
a manufacturing defect is claimed. Accepted defect claims include separation of the
product, contamination, an off odour, incorrect fill volume, or a broken seal on
arrival. Dissatisfaction with fragrance or texture is not a defect and is not
returnable once the seal is broken.

## 3. Non-returnable categories

The following are never accepted for return: opened cosmetics used beyond a patch test,
gift sets with any component missing, clearance items marked final sale, and products
purchased with a promotional code marked non-returnable at checkout.

## 4. Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days of the
returned item being scanned at the Aurora warehouse. Store credit, where the customer
elects it, is issued within 24 hours of the same scan. UPI and card refunds in India
typically settle in 3 to 5 working days after Aurora issues them; Malaysian FPX refunds
settle in 5 to 10 working days. Aurora cannot accelerate settlement once the refund has
been issued, as the remaining delay sits with the issuing bank.

## 5. Refunds where the item is not returned

Where Aurora waives the return — for example a leaking bottle that cannot be shipped
safely — the refund is issued on approval by a care supervisor without waiting for a
warehouse scan. The customer is asked to dispose of the item responsibly.

## 6. Partial refunds

Partial refunds apply where only some lines of an order are returned. Shipping charges
are refunded in full only when the entire order is returned, or when the return arises
from an Aurora error such as a wrong or damaged item.

## 7. Exchange in place of refund

Customers may request an exchange rather than a refund within the same windows above.
Exchanges are dispatched once the returned item is scanned, except where the customer is
Platinum segment, in which case the replacement is dispatched immediately on approval
and the return is collected in parallel.
