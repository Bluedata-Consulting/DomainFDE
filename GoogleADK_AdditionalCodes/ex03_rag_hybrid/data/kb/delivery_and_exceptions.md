# Delivery Commitments and Exception Handling

## 1. Delivery commitments

Standard metro delivery is 2 to 4 working days from dispatch. Non-metro delivery is 4 to
7 working days. Same-day delivery is offered in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru and Kuala
Lumpur for orders placed before 11:00 local time, subject to store stock.

Working days exclude Sundays and declared public holidays in the destination country.
The promised delivery date shown at checkout already accounts for these.

## 2. Lost in transit

A parcel is declared lost when there has been no carrier scan movement for 7 consecutive
days past the promised delivery date. On declaration, the customer is entitled to a free
replacement or a full refund, at the customer's choice. Aurora does not require the
customer to file a carrier complaint first.

Where a parcel is scanned as delivered but the customer states it was not received,
Aurora opens a proof-of-delivery dispute with the carrier. The customer is not made to
wait for the dispute outcome: resolution is issued within 48 hours of the claim, and
Aurora recovers from the carrier separately.

## 3. Damaged on arrival

Damage claims require photographs submitted within 48 hours of the delivery scan. With
photographs, a replacement is dispatched automatically with no supervisor approval. Where
photographs are not available, replacement requires care supervisor approval, which is
granted by default for customers with no prior claim in the preceding 12 months.

Where leakage from one item has damaged others in the same parcel, the entire order is
replaced, not only the leaking item.

## 4. Short shipment

A short shipment claim is verified against the warehouse pack weight recorded at
dispatch. Where the recorded weight is consistent with the missing line being absent,
the claim is accepted and the missing lines are replaced. Where the weight is consistent
with a complete pack, the claim is escalated to the fulfilment investigations queue and
the customer is told a decision will follow within 3 working days.

## 5. Wrong item despatched

Aurora arranges free reverse pickup and dispatches the correct item immediately, without
waiting for the wrong item to be returned. The customer is never asked to pay return
shipping on an Aurora picking error.

## 6. Failed delivery

Carriers attempt delivery three times. Where all three fail because the customer was
unavailable or unreachable, this is not an Aurora fault: redelivery is offered at no
charge but no goodwill credit is issued. Where the failure is caused by an incomplete
address that Aurora captured incorrectly, it is treated as an Aurora error and the
customer is entitled to redelivery plus goodwill credit under the goodwill policy.

## 7. Cross-border orders

Malaysian orders fulfilled from Indian inventory carry an additional 3 to 5 working days
for customs clearance. Customs delays are excluded from the lost-in-transit clock; the
7-day count starts only after the customs-cleared scan.
