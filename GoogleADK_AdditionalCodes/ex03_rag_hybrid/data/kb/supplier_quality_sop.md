# Supplier Quality and Product Quarantine SOP

## 1. Purpose

This SOP governs how Aurora Retail places a product batch under quarantine, what
happens to stock and open orders during quarantine, and how the batch is released or
withdrawn.

## 2. Quarantine triggers

A batch is placed under quarantine within 4 hours when any of the following occur:

- Three or more adverse reaction reports against the same SKU within 30 days.
- A supplier-initiated recall notice, whether voluntary or regulator-directed.
- A failed retained-sample test at the contracted laboratory.
- A confirmed packaging integrity failure affecting more than 2 percent of a batch.
- A regulatory query from FSSAI, CDSCO, or Malaysia's NPRA relating to the batch.

## 3. Effect of quarantine on stock

Quarantined stock is blocked at every node simultaneously: distribution centres, store
back rooms and in-transit inventory. The SKU is delisted from the web channel within 30
minutes of quarantine. Store point-of-sale blocks the barcode at the till.

Quarantined stock must never be used to fulfil a replacement, an exchange, or a goodwill
gesture. Where a replacement was promised before quarantine, the commitment converts to
a full refund and the customer is informed within one working day.

## 4. Open orders during quarantine

Orders containing a quarantined SKU that have not yet dispatched are cancelled and
refunded in full, including shipping. Orders already in transit are intercepted where the
carrier supports it; where interception fails, the customer is contacted before delivery
with instructions not to use the product, and a reverse pickup is arranged.

## 5. Batch release

A quarantined batch is released only on written sign-off from the Head of Quality
Assurance, supported by a passing retest from the contracted laboratory and a supplier
corrective action report. Release restores the SKU to sale at all nodes within 2 working
hours.

## 6. Batch withdrawal

Where release is not justified, the batch is withdrawn. Withdrawn stock is segregated,
recorded against the supplier's quality ledger, and destroyed or returned per the supply
agreement. Withdrawal triggers a supplier chargeback covering landed cost, reverse
logistics, customer refunds and goodwill credit issued on affected orders.

## 7. Supplier performance thresholds

Two withdrawals in a rolling 12-month period place the supplier on probation, with all
inbound batches subject to pre-acceptance testing at the supplier's cost. A third places
the supplier under review for delisting.
