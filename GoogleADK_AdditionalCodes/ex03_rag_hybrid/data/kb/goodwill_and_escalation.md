# Goodwill Credit and Escalation Matrix

## 1. Goodwill principles

Goodwill credit acknowledges inconvenience. It is not compensation for the value of the
order, which is handled separately through refund or replacement. Goodwill is issued as
Aurora store credit valid for 12 months, never as cash.

## 2. Goodwill ceilings

Goodwill credit is capped at 10 percent of order value for a first service failure. The
cap rises to 15 percent where the customer has experienced a prior service failure on
any order within the preceding 90 days. Any credit above these ceilings is a policy
exception and requires care supervisor sign-off before it is communicated to the
customer.

Care agents may issue goodwill up to INR 500 without approval. Team leads may issue up
to INR 2,000. Anything above INR 2,000 requires a care manager.

## 3. When goodwill is not issued

No goodwill is issued for: delivery failures caused by customer unavailability after
three attempts, delays attributable to declared public holidays or weather events,
refused deliveries, or claims found to be inconsistent with carrier records.

## 4. Escalation matrix

The following are escalated to a human supervisor before any reply is sent to the
customer, regardless of what automation recommends:

- Any claim of an adverse reaction, allergy, injury or illness attributed to a product.
- Any suspected counterfeit or tampered product.
- Any product under an active quality quarantine.
- Any threat of legal action, regulatory complaint or consumer forum filing.
- Any complaint published on a public social channel with named reference to Aurora.
- Any request involving a customer's personal data beyond order status.
- Any order above INR 25,000 in value.

## 5. Adverse reaction handling

Adverse reaction reports are logged to the product safety register within 4 hours and
routed to the quality team the same working day. The care response acknowledges the
report, does not admit or deny causation, provides the full ingredient list on request,
and commits to a senior response within one working day. Care staff must not offer a
refund or goodwill credit in the first response to an adverse reaction, as this can be
read as an admission.

## 6. Repeat failure handling

A second service failure on the same customer within 90 days is routed to the retention
desk regardless of value. The retention desk may authorise goodwill beyond standard
ceilings and is the only function permitted to offer a service-recovery gesture outside
the goodwill framework.
