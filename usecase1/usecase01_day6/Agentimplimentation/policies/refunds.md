# Refund policy

Owner: Billing team lead. Applies to every complaint about an order.

## What a refund is
A refund is money returned for the order a complaint is about. It is not compensation, a goodwill payment, a discount or a voucher.

## Refund limits
- The complaints service may refund up to 25 pounds in total per complaint, across all refunds, without approval.
- Any refund that would take the total above 25 pounds needs approval from the Billing team lead before anything is paid.
- The total of all refunds on a complaint may never exceed the value of the order.

## Timing
Approved refunds are paid within 5 working days of approval.
