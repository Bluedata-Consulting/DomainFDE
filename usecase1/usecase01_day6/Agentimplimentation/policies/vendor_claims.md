# Vendor claims policy

Owner: Returns supervisor.

## When to claim
Raise a vendor claim for any grade C item that is faulty because of the manufacturer, while the vendor's claim window is still open.

## Claim windows
Each vendor gives a claim window, counted in days from the date the item is received back. A claim raised after the window closes is rejected by the vendor, and the cost is written off.

## Evidence
Every claim needs the inspector's grade and a short description of the fault.
