# Returns grading and disposition policy

Owner: Returns supervisor. Applies to every item returned to the returns centre.

## Condition grades
The inspector grades every returned item. The grade cannot be changed by anyone except another inspector.
- Grade A: as new, in original packaging, fully working. May be restocked.
- Grade B: opened or lightly used, fully working. Refurbish or liquidate.
- Grade C: damaged or faulty. Never restocked. Return to vendor if a vendor claim is open, otherwise liquidate or recycle.

## Dispositions
The possible dispositions are restock, refurbish, return to vendor, liquidate, recycle and quarantine.

## Recalled items
An item on the recall list may only be quarantined, whatever its grade. It is never restocked, liquidated or recycled until the recall notice says how.

## High-value items
Any decision on an item worth more than 500 pounds is made by the returns supervisor.

## Liquidation recovery
Liquidated items are sold to a clearance partner. Typical recovery is 15 to 25 per cent of cost for small electricals and 10 to 15 per cent for furniture.
