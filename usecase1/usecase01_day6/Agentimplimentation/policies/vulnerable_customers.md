# Vulnerable customers and legal matters

Owner: Complaints team lead.

## Who is a vulnerable customer
A customer whose circumstances mean they need specialist handling, for example a full-time carer, a customer in bereavement, or a customer with a serious illness. Vulnerability belongs to the person, and applies on every account they hold.

## What happens
Any complaint from a vulnerable customer, or any complaint that mentions an ombudsman, a solicitor or legal action, is handed to the complaints team lead. The agent may send a short service acknowledgement, and nothing else.
