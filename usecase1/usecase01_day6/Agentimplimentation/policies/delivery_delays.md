# Delivery delay policy

Owner: Complaints team lead.

## Apologies
Every late delivery gets a service apology on a channel the customer has agreed to.

## Goodwill credit for long delays
- There is no automatic compensation for late delivery.
- For a delivery more than 5 days late, the complaints team lead may approve a goodwill credit of up to 5 pounds, applied to the customer's account.
- The agent may not offer or promise a goodwill credit. It may tell the customer that the team lead will review the delay.

## Delays of 2 days or fewer
No credit applies. An apology and an updated delivery date are enough.
