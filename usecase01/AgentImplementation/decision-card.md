## 2. Autonomy sort

Levels: **L0** assist, **L1** advise, **L2** propose (a human approves),
**L3** act and notify, **L4** act alone.
Cynefin: **complicated** (rules can check it) or **complex** (needs judgement).

Ask of every row: *if this goes wrong, how fast would we know, and could we undo it?*
Those two answers decide the level. Nothing else does.
