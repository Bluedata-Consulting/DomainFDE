INC-2024-11 — Belawan port closure and Nightwatch substitution
Severity: 4 Opened: 6 November 2024 Closed: 3 December 2024 Owner: Head of Quality
Summary
A port closure at Belawan delayed the Sumatran green coffee that forms the base of
Nightwatch Blend by 23 days. With no Sumatran green in the silo and a scheduled roast,
production substituted a Guatemalan lot into the base position of Nightwatch Blend across
eleven roast batches between 12 and 27 November.
The substitute lot scored 81.2 against a Nightwatch base specification minimum of 82.0, a
delta of 0.8 against specification — but the lot previously in use had scored 85.1, so the delta
as customers experienced it was 3.9 points. The resulting cup was materially different: less
body, more acidity, none of the earthy character Nightwatch drinkers buy it for.
