confirmation, or where the complaint concerns a matter of taste preference rather than
product defect.
Step 2 — Replacement. Replacement product despatched at no charge, on the next
available despatch. Appropriate for a single defective bag, a wrong item shipped, or a grind
error. Replacement is preferred over credit wherever the customer's underlying want is
coffee rather than money.
Step 3 — Partial credit. A credit against the order value, typically between twenty-five and
fifty per cent. Appropriate where the customer received usable product that fell short of
specification, or where a delay materially reduced the value of the product without
destroying it.
Step 4 — Full refund plus goodwill. Full order value returned, plus a goodwill credit
toward a future order. Appropriate where the product was unusable, where a service failure
was repeated, or where the attribution sits squarely inside Meridian.

5. Goodwill credit caps
The following caps apply to the goodwill component only. They are the maximum a remedy
may reach before any uplift under §7 is applied.

5.1 Direct-to-consumer
| Tier | Standard cap (USD) |
|---|---|
| Bronze | 25 |
| Silver | 50 |
| Gold | 120 |

5.2 Wholesale
| Tier | Standard cap (USD) |
|---|---|
| Trial | 100 |
| Standard | 250 |
| Flagship | 500 |

5.3 Reading the caps correctly
The cap is a ceiling, not a target. Most cases resolve well below it. An agent that offers the
cap by default is not applying judgement and will be picked up in case review.
