Standing lesson
This incident had a supply cause, a quality consequence and a customer consequence, and each was visible to a different team. None of the three teams could see the whole shape of it. The delay was known twenty-seven days before the first complaint was understood.
The controls that would have caught it — an automated notification trigger and complaint-rate alerting — are action items 3 and 4, and both remain open.

INC-2025-04 — Decaf mislabelling
Severity: 3 Opened: 18 April 2025 Closed: 29 April 2025 Owner: Production
Summary
A packaging run applied Quietwater Decaf labels to approximately 200 bags of regular Cascadia Blend following a label reel change on the wrong line.
Root cause
Human error at line changeover, with no verification step between label load and run start.
Customer impact
47 customers received caffeinated coffee labelled as decaf. This is a consumer safety matter, not merely a quality one. All affected customers were contacted within 24 hours of discovery with an explicit instruction not to consume, and a full refund plus replacement was issued regardless of tier.
Action items
| # | Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Barcode verification at label load, blocking run start on mismatch | Production | Closed Jul 2025 |
| 2 | Decaf runs physically segregated to a dedicated line | Production | Closed Jun 2025 |

Note
The remedy here was issued without reference to caps, correctly. Safety overrides the remedy ladder. MR-CC-POL-004 §1 carries this forward.
