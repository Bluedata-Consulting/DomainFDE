Green Coffee Quality Standards & Roasting QA Manual
Meridian Roasters Co. Document reference: MR-QA-STD-002 Version: 2.3 Effective: 1
September 2025 Owner: Head of Quality Applies to: green coffee intake, lot release, blend
composition, roast production, finished-goods release Review cycle: annual

1. Purpose
This manual defines the quality standards that green coffee must meet to be accepted, the
specifications each product must be built to, the conditions under which a component may
be substituted, and the records that must exist before finished goods are released for sale.
It is written for the quality team, the roasting team and production planning. Where it
conflicts with a production schedule, this manual takes precedence and the schedule
moves.

2. Cupping protocol
2.1 Method
All green coffee is evaluated on arrival using the SCA cupping protocol, ten cups per lot,
three assessors, scored independently before discussion. Scores are recorded to one
decimal place against the lot record.
Where assessors disagree by more than two points, a fourth assessor is brought in and the
lot is rescored. The original scores are retained; they are not overwritten.

2.2 Score bands
| Band | Score | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Exceptional | 88.0 and above | Single-origin release; micro-lot programme eligible |
| Speciality | 84.0 to 87.9 | Single-origin release; blend base eligible |
| Premium | 82.0 to 83.9 | Blend component only; not released as single origin |
| Commercial | 80.0 to 81.9 | Blend filler only, maximum thirty per cent of a blend by weight |
| Reject | Below 80.0 | Not released. Quarantine and supplier claim under MR-PROC-HB-003 §7 |

2.3 Score is a property of the lot, not the supplier
A supplier who consistently delivers at 86 has not thereby earned an 86 on an unscored lot.
Every lot is scored on arrival. No lot enters production on the basis of the supplier's history.
