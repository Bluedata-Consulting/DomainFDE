Company-attributable. Any failure originating inside Meridian, including production
error, quality-control failure, blend specification deviation, mis-pick, roasting fault,
inventory error, or a procurement failure that propagated into the product. Uplift under §7.3
applies.
Mixed or undetermined. Where two independent causes affected the same customer, or
where attribution cannot be established on the evidence available. Treated as company-
attributable for remedy purposes, on the principle that the customer should not bear the
cost of our uncertainty. The case must be flagged for root-cause review.
7.2 Attribution is evidence-based
Attribution must be supported by evidence from the operational record, not inferred from
the customer's description. A customer reporting that coffee "tasted stale" has not
established attribution; the batch record, the lot record and the quality note have.
Where a complaint concerns product quality, attribution requires tracing the affected order
line back to its roast batch and green lot. Where a complaint concerns delivery, attribution
requires the shipment exception record and, where a weather or holiday cause is claimed,
external corroboration.
7.3 Company-attributable root cause uplift
Where attribution is company-attributable or mixed, the applicable goodwill cap rises by
one band:

| Channel | Standard band | Uplifted band |
| :-------------- | :------------ | :------------ |
| D2C bronze | 25 | 50 |
| D2C silver | 50 | 120 |
| D2C gold | 120 | 250 |
| Wholesale trial | 100 | 250 |
| Wholesale standard | 250 | 500 |
| Wholesale flagship | 500 | 1,250 |

The uplift does not change the approval matrix in §6. A gold customer uplifted to 250 still
requires supervisor approval above 150.
