10. Remedy ladder for supplier breach
| Step | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First breach in rolling 12 months | Written notice, recorded on supplier record |
| 2 | Second breach | Formal performance conversation, corrective action plan requested |
| 3 | Third breach, or second at a sole- source supplier | Supplier review under §11 |
| 4 | Review outcome | Remediation plan, volume reduction, or notice to terminate |

11. Supplier review triggers
11.1 Sole-source suppliers
Where a supplier is the only qualified source for a component, review is triggered by two
breaches within a rolling twelve months, rather than three. The lower threshold reflects
that a sole-source failure has no mitigation and propagates directly into product.
Review of a sole-source supplier must consider, and record, the following before any
recommendation is made:
*   breach history with variance for each breach
*   whether any breach was relieved under §9, and if so on what evidence
*   downstream quality impact, including any substitution made under MR-QA-STD-002
    §5 as a consequence
*   customer impact and remedy cost arising
*   the state of alternate source qualification
*   the practical consequence of termination given no alternate exists
A recommendation to terminate a sole-source supplier without a qualified alternate in place
is a recommendation to discontinue the affected product. That is a commercial decision for
the Commercial Director and the Managing Director jointly, not a procurement decision,
and it must be presented as the product decision it actually is.

11.2 Multi-source suppliers
Three breaches within a rolling twelve months, or two quality claims, or any single breach
exceeding thirty days' variance.
