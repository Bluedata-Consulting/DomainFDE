10. Precedent log
Cases recorded for consistency. Precedent is persuasive, not binding.
PR-2024-03. Gold D2C subscriber, three consecutive deliveries arriving past promise
during a regional carrier dispute. Third-party-attributable. Standard gold cap applied at 120,
no uplift. Customer accepted. Upheld on review.
PR-2024-09. Standard wholesale account, espresso blend outside specification across a
two-week production window. Company-attributable. Uplifted to 500 under the then-draft
§7.3. Account retained. This case motivated the formal adoption of §7.3 in v4.0.
PR-2025-02. Flagship wholesale account, single late delivery caused by a port closure.
Third-party-attributable despite the account's insistence otherwise. Standard flagship cap
of 500 applied. Reviewed and upheld; external cause does not become internal because the
customer is important.
PR-2025-07. Bronze D2C customer, mis-picked order. Company-attributable, uplifted to 50.
Customer requested a larger credit citing inconvenience. Declined and upheld; tier and
attribution set the band, customer expectation does not.
