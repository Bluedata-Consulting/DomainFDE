3. Customer tiers
3.1 Direct-to-consumer loyalty tiers
| Tier | Qualification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | Default on account creation | Includes all trial and one-off purchasers |
| Silver | Six completed orders, or an active subscription running twelve weeks | Tier is evaluated nightly |
| Gold | Eighteen completed orders, or an active subscription running fifty-two weeks, or trailing twelve-month spend above USD 600 | Tier is sticky for ninety days after qualification lapses |

Tier is a property of the customer record and must be read from the customer profile at the time of the decision. It must not be inferred from order volume in the conversation, because sticky-tier customers will look under-qualified.

3.2 Wholesale account tiers
| Tier | Qualification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Trial | First ninety days from onboarding | No standing remedy authority; every case goes to the account manager |
| Standard | Post-trial, annual contract value below USD 40,000 | |
| Flagship | Annual contract value at or above USD 40,000, or designated by the Commercial Director irrespective of value | Designation is reviewed annually and may be held for strategic reasons |

Flagship designation is not purely numerical. Accounts may be designated flagship because they are reference customers, because they occupy a strategic city, or because they influence a wider buying group. The designation is recorded on the account record and is authoritative.

4. The standard remedy ladder
Remedies escalate through four steps. An agent should identify the correct step and offer it directly rather than climbing the ladder in conversation.

Step 1 — Acknowledgement and explanation. No financial value. Appropriate where the customer experienced a minor delay within the tolerance published on the order
