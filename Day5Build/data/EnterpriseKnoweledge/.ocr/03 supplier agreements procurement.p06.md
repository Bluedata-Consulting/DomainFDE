Variance is assessed per purchase order. A supplier's average variance across a year can sit
comfortably inside tolerance while individual orders breach badly, and it is individual
breaches that damage production. Average variance is a relationship health indicator;
individual breach count is the contractual measure. Reporting that shows only the average
will conceal exactly the events this handbook exists to manage.
4.3 Worked example
A purchase order raised on a supplier with a 42-day contracted target and ±7 tolerance,
arriving 68 days after the purchase order date, has an actual lead time of 68 days and a
variance of +26 days. This exceeds the tolerance band by 19 days and is a breach unless
relieved under §9.
5. Quality claims
Where a delivered lot scores below the contractual quality floor, a claim is raised within
fourteen days of cupping. Remedies available, in order of preference:
1. Replacement at supplier cost on the next available sailing
2. Price adjustment proportionate to the score shortfall
3. Rejection and full credit
A lot in the Reject band under MR-QA-STD-002 §2.2 is rejected outright and is not
negotiated down into a blend.
Claims are tracked against the supplier record and count toward the review triggers in §11.
6. Payment and currency
Payment terms are net 30 from bill of lading date, in USD, against documents. FX exposure
sits between contract price in USD and revenue in USD, CAD, GBP and EUR, and is reported
monthly by Finance.
Procurement does not hedge. Where a currency movement materially changes landed cost,
that is reported rather than managed at the purchase order level.
7. Supplier quality claims and counterclaims
Suppliers may claim against Meridian for wrongful rejection, late payment, or failure to
take contracted volume. Counterclaims are handled by Legal and are recorded on the
supplier record so that any review under §11 sees both directions of the relationship. A
supplier with three breaches against us and two legitimate counterclaims against them is a
different conversation from one with three breaches and a clean record.
8. Ordering and call-off discipline
Quarterly call-offs must be raised at least one full lead time plus tolerance before the
required arrival date. A purchase order raised late produces a late arrival that is not a
supplier breach, and misclassifying our own planning failure as a supplier breach corrupts
the review process at §11.
