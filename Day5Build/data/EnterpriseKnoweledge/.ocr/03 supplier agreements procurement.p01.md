1. Purpose
This handbook summarises the commercial terms Meridian Roasters holds with each
green coffee supplier, explains how performance against those terms is measured, and
defines the process by which a supplier relationship is reviewed, remediated or ended.
Contract summaries in §3 are abstracts. The executed agreements are held by Legal and
prevail in any dispute. Where a summary here and an executed contract differ, the contract
governs and this handbook is wrong and must be corrected.

2. How we buy
Meridian buys green coffee on fixed-price forward contracts, denominated in USD, on FOB
terms from origin port. We take title at the origin port rail and carry the freight, insurance
and arrival risk from that point.
This matters for how delay is treated. Because we are the party arranging carriage from
FOB, an ocean delay after loading is ordinarily our risk, not the supplier's. The exceptions
are set out in §4.3 and are the subject of specific negotiated carve-outs with two of our five
suppliers.
Purchase orders are raised against an annual volume commitment with quarterly call-offs.
Lead time runs from purchase order date to arrival at the Port of Portland, and is the
measure used throughout this handbook.
