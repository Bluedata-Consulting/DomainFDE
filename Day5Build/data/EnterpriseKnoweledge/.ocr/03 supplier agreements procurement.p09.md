11.3 What a review cannot settle
A supplier review assembles evidence. It does not, by itself, answer whether a relationship
should end. That question weighs contractual performance against switching cost,
qualification lead time, relationship history, origin availability and product strategy, and
several of those inputs are judgements held outside procurement.
Reviews that present a termination recommendation as though it followed arithmetically
from the breach count have overstepped, and are returned.
