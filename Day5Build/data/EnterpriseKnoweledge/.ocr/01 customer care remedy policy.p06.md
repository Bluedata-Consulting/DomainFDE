7.5 Repeat-incident multiplier
Where the same customer experiences the same failure mode three or more times within
ninety days, the case must be escalated to the Director of Customer Experience irrespective
of value, and the underlying failure raised as a systemic issue. Repeat failures are a process
defect, and remedying them individually conceals the defect.

8. Cross-currency handling
Wholesale accounts are invoiced in their contracted currency. Caps in this document are
expressed in USD.
Conversion must be performed before comparison against a cap and before any
aggregation across accounts. A CAD amount compared directly against a USD cap will
systematically over-issue. Use the rate published for the incident date, not the rate on the
day the decision is taken, and record the rate used against the case.
Aggregate exposure reporting must convert every component to USD before summing.
Summing mixed-currency values and converting the total is not permitted and has
produced material misstatement in the past.

9. Communication standards
Remedy communications must:
• name the cause plainly, in the customer's language rather than internal terminology,
without disclosing supplier names, lot identifiers, batch numbers or commercial terms
• state what is being offered and what happens next, with a date
• avoid the passive voice when describing our own failures
• avoid offering an explanation the evidence does not support, including plausible-
sounding causes that have not been established
