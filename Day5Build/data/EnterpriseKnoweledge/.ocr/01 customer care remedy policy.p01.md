1. Purpose and scope
This policy governs every financial or material remedy offered to a Meridian Roasters
customer, across both the direct-to-consumer and wholesale channels. It defines who may
authorise what, under which conditions, and to what value.
It applies to:
• Goodwill credits and account credits
• Full and partial refunds
• Replacement product despatched at no charge
• Subscription extensions and free cycles
• Wholesale credit notes and invoice adjustments
• Relationship recovery packages under §7.4
It does not govern statutory refund obligations, which are handled under MR-LEG-POL-002
and always take precedence over anything written here. Where a customer has a statutory
right to a remedy, that right is honoured in full regardless of the caps in this document, and
the difference is booked to the statutory remedy cost centre rather than to goodwill.

2. Principles
Four principles govern every decision made under this policy.
Proportionality. The remedy should be proportionate to the harm, not to the volume of the
complaint. A calm customer who has suffered a genuine product failure is owed more than
a loud customer who has suffered an inconvenience.
Attribution honesty. The remedy band depends on who caused the problem. Where the
fault lies inside Meridian, the customer should not be worse off than if the fault had lain
with a carrier. §7.3 exists because that was not always true under v3.1.
Single touch. A customer should receive one remedy decision, not an escalating sequence
of small offers. Agents are expected to determine the correct remedy once and offer it in
full. Incremental bidding damages trust more than a slightly low first offer.
Documented rationale. Every remedy above the Tier 1 threshold requires a written
rationale recorded against the case. "Customer was unhappy" is not a rationale. The
rationale must name the fault, the attribution and the clause applied.
