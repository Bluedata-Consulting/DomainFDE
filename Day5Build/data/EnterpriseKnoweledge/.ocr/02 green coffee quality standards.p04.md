5.3 Score delta
Score delta is the difference between the cupping score of the specified component and the
cupping score of the substitute. It is calculated against the specification minimum, not
against the score of the lot that happened to be in use last week.
Example. Cascadia Blend base carries a specification minimum of 84.0. A substitute lot
scoring 79.5 gives a delta of 4.5, which is above the 2.0 threshold, requires joint QA Lead and
Head of Quality authority under §5.2, and triggers the notification obligation in §5.4.
A substitute scoring below 80.0 is in the Reject band under §2.2 and may not be used in any
blend regardless of authority. A substitute between 80.0 and 81.9 is Commercial band and
may not exceed thirty per cent of the blend by weight, which disqualifies it from any base
position.
5.4 Customer notification obligation
Where a substitution produces a score delta greater than 2.0 points against
specification, affected customers must be notified before or with despatch.
The notification must:
• state that the product differs from its usual composition
• state the nature of the difference in plain terms
• offer the customer the option to decline the shipment or to substitute a different
product at no charge
• be issued to every customer whose order contains the affected batch, including
wholesale accounts
The obligation sits with the Head of Quality to raise and with Customer Experience to
execute. It is not discretionary and it is not waived by a judgement that the cup is "probably
fine". A customer who discovers the substitution by tasting it has been denied a choice they
were entitled to make.
Failure to notify where notification was required is a quality incident in its own right,
independent of whether any customer complains, and must be raised as such.
5.5 Batch record requirements
Every substitution must be recorded on the batch record with:
• the specified component and the substitute used
• both cupping scores and the calculated delta
• the authorising individual and the authority level applied
• whether notification was required under §5.4, and if so, the date it issued
• the reason the specified component was unavailable
