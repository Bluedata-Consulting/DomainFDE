How to read this archive
Every incident above severity 3 gets a post-mortem within ten working days. The format is
fixed: summary, timeline, root cause, contributing factors, customer impact, what we did,
and action items with an owner and a status.
Action item status is the most important field in this document. An open action item
from eighteen months ago is not history, it is a live risk, and it is the reason most repeat
incidents repeat.

Severity scale:
| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Single customer, contained, no systemic signal |
| 2 | Multiple customers, one product or one route, resolved within a week |
| 3 | Multiple customers across a product line, or any incident with a quality-specification deviation |
| 4 | Material revenue or relationship risk, or a control failure |
| 5 | Regulatory, safety, or existential |

INC-2024-03 — Carrier sortation failure, Pacific Northwest
Severity: 2 Opened: 11 March 2024 Closed: 22 March 2024 Owner: Fulfilment Operations

Summary
Approximately 90 parcels routed through a carrier sortation hub in Tacoma were held for
six to nine days following a facility systems failure at the carrier. Customers in Washington
and Oregon were affected.

Timeline
* 9 Mar — Carrier facility systems failure, not notified to shippers
* 11 Mar — First customer contacts received; tracking showing no movement
* 12 Mar — Carrier confirms facility issue on escalation
* 14 Mar — Affected parcel list assembled; proactive notification issued
